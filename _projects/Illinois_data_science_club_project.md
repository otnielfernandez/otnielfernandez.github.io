A project that placed second in the annual Illinois Data Science Club competition. This project explored greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions in the city of Chicago. The goal was to evaluate how effective the city's energy policies have been at reducing environmental impact and to uncover key trends in their energy use. Specifically, we explored 3 key topics: What is the relationship between GHG emissions and factors like electricity usage and building size? What are the differences in GHG usage across different building types? What is the overall efficacy of city policies aimed at reducing emissions? Some key methodologies used in this project included predictive modeling, hypothesis testing, and data cleaning in Python.
